# Steno 1.1 design record

This record describes the design Steno 1.1 ships across the dictation island, the main window, the menu bar, Settings, the setup guide and the app icon. It gives each decision and the reason for it, and the looks that were tried and dropped. The source code is the authority. Where a value below names the file that defines it, a value that disagrees with its file is wrong here, not there.

An earlier candidate design for 1.1 was built and replaced by this one before release.

## Decisions in brief

- **Green is the only identity.** One forest green carries the brand, the accent and the color of live voice. A second, blue identity was built and removed: one identity gives one icon, one set of renders and less to test. The saved accent preference stays on disk and is not read.
- **Glass is the only island and live-stage style.** Forest glass stands out against white and dark documents alike, and it makes the island and Today's live stage read as one surface. Paper and dot islands and paper and tint stages were built and removed.
- **Grounds are neutral.** The window is white in light and near-black in dark. Green is the accent and the color of live voice; it never tints the window.
- **No ruled lines.** Panels are separated from the ground by one soft edge around the whole panel, never by lines between rows.
- **Rows show only exceptions.** A dictation that was inserted normally shows no chip. Only exceptions are named: copied and why, pasted, not inserted, fixed.
- **The voice quill is the mark.** One definition draws the app icon, the Dock tile, the menu bar icon, the island's mark and every quill in the window (`BrandMark.swift`).
- **What answers a key press is instant.** The island capsule, the start of a dictation and the live stage's changes do not animate, because they happen many times a day. Only the notch form grows, out of the screen edge it belongs to. Otherwise motion is kept for results and for leaving: the island changing width, the notch retracting, the stage folding into its card.

## Looks considered and rejected

| Considered | Why it was dropped |
| --- | --- |
| A paper transcript page: sheet, line numbers, ruled lines, a fixed measure | Rejected as generic after use in a built version. Dictations now flow to the width of their card in the system font. |
| A blue or indigo palette | Rejected for the same reason; replaced by forest green. |
| Green and blue as two equal identities | Two icons, two sets of renders and twice the testing for one product. Green only. |
| Cream, parchment, tan or ivory grounds | Read as a template look. Grounds are clean neutrals. |
| A window tinted green, an oversized bold page title, a heavy dark selected pill with a shadow, a two-tone sidebar, dashboard stat cards, chips on every row | All tried in an early prototype and dropped. The window keeps the forest for live voice; the selected row is a neutral fill; Today's heading is the date at 15 pt; Insights opens with a sentence, not stat cards. |
| Neon citron accents | Rejected as a look. The design has one green accent. |
| A dot island | It cut the start of the live words and dropped the words from its result card. |
| Paper island, paper and tint live stages | Paper and tint grounds had already been rejected; glass reads on any document. |
| Global Esc to discard a recording | A global Esc would also act in the app being dictated into. The island takes no keyboard focus; discard is its × and the menu's Discard. |
| A card that shortened a long dictation as its edge was dragged up | The dictation sat in a card inside the day panel with a handle on its edge and a read time under it. In use the nested card and the handle read as clutter, so the feature was removed before release. Dictations show as plain text. |
| Respelling configured names from the audio after decoding | Measured on 35 near-miss name pairs read by six voices, it wrote configured names nobody said. Corrections are explicit only: Fix a Word records a replacement the person typed. |

Marks that came too close to existing logos, read as script or an emoticon, or crowded at small sizes were dropped in favor of the voice quill. The nib has a single slit and no round breather hole, so it reads as a cut quill rather than the fountain-pen nib common among writing apps (`BrandMark.swift`).

## Type

The windows and the island set every line in the system font (`TranscriptLayout.swift`). In the island the words, the app name, Copy, Open Settings and the ⌘V keycap are the system face, and the recording time and countdown are the system face with tabular digits. Sizes at the default text size:

| Use | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Dictation text | 22 pt up to 140 characters, 17 pt up to 500, then 15 pt and 12 lines until Show All |
| Dictation line height | 1.36 of the size at 20 pt and up, 1.45 from 16 pt, 1.5 below |
| Live stage words | 22 pt, up to four lines |
| Insights opening sentence | 22 pt |
| Today's date heading | 15 pt semibold |
| List rows | 13 pt; the app and time line under them 11 pt |
| Section labels | 12 to 12.5 pt semibold, tertiary ink |
| Buttons | 13 pt (12 pt small) |
| Island words | 12 pt; verbs semibold, notices medium, the countdown in tabular digits |
| Island live words | 13 pt, or the body size when larger, on a 19:13 line |
| Settings | titles 20 pt semibold, body 13 pt, captions 12 pt |

Dictation line height is set in `TranscriptLayout.swift`, the island's live words in `WaveformOverlayPresenter.swift`, and the Settings sizes in `SettingsStyle.swift`.

The windows follow the text size set in System Settings › Accessibility › Display › Text Size. The setting is read from the body font's size, mapped to the nearest step, never below the default, and read again whenever Steno becomes active (`SystemTextSize.swift`). Window text grows up to 1.35 times, the largest step the layouts hold. The island scales its text with the body size, and its shapes grow at 0.8 of that rate so larger text does not double the capsule (`WaveformOverlayPresenter.swift`).

## The voice quill

The quill is a curved shaft ending in a cut nib, with dense barbs on both sides. The barbs on the outer, lower-right side carry the voice: at rest their lengths trace a spoken rhythm, and while Steno listens they follow the live input level (`BrandMark.swift`).

- **Two cuts.** The full cut has 36 barb slots a side with two left empty as the feather's natural splits, and is used from 48 pixels up. The small cut is a filled vane whose outer edge carries the voice in 12 segments over a heavier shaft, for the 16 and 32 pixel icons, the 18 pt menu bar template and the island's 22 pt glyph.
- **Motion.** Listening: the outer barbs move to new lengths with each level update, easing over 0.12 s, the tip reading older levels so the voice travels from nib to tip. Transcribing: the barbs settle over 0.35 s and the nib writes a line along the baseline in a 2.2 s loop. Result: the line completes over 0.35 s and holds. The inner vane never moves (`BrandMark.swift`, `BrandMarkView.swift`).
- **Reduce Motion.** The barbs never move. Listening shows the level by the barbs' opacity, at most ten times a second; writing dims the quill; the result fades it to full (`BrandMarkView.swift`, `NibLayer.swift`).
- **Where it appears.** The app icon and the About panel; the Dock tile, drawn at runtime from the same definition; the menu bar icon; the island while transcribing and on a result; Today's Dictate stage and live stage; the empty states of Today and History; the last step of the setup guide; the foot of the Settings sidebar (`MenuBarIcon.swift`, `WaveformOverlayPresenter.swift`).
- **The icon.** The tile is the 824 square on Apple's 1024 grid with a 185 radius at 1024; the glyph fills 86% of the tile, 96% for the small cut (`BrandIconArtwork.swift`, `BrandMark.swift`). `StenoIconRenderer` writes the bundled icon set and the Icon Composer layers from the same code, so the bundle's icon and the Dock's match.

## Surfaces

### Island

The island is the overlay that shows while Steno listens, transcribes and reports a result. It appears on every dictation, over whatever app is in front, so it is small, instant and dark over any document.

**Placement** (`WaveformOverlayPresenter.swift`)

- It grows out of the notch only when exactly one display is online and it is a built-in display with a notch. Mirrored and Sidecar displays count as external.
- Otherwise it is a capsule, top centre, 12 pt below the menu bar, on the display that holds most of the front app's frontmost window when the dictation starts. When that app has no window on screen, it uses the display under the pointer (`DictationController.swift`).
- In the notch the neck is exactly the notch's width down to the menu bar's bottom edge, then widens through 10 pt concave shoulders. Nothing above the menu bar's edge is wider than the notch, and only the body below it casts a shadow, so the menu bar items beside the notch stay clear and undarkened.
- The window sits above the menu bar and below open menus, on every Space and beside full-screen apps. It never becomes key, so it never takes the keyboard.

**Sizes** (`WaveformOverlayPresenter.swift`). The capsule is 34 pt tall and the row below the notch 32 pt. The island is at most 412 pt wide. Live words, when that setting is on, grow the same shape downward to at most three lines, inside an 18 pt corner radius.

**What each state shows** (`OverlayPresentation.swift`)

| State | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Holding the key | Mint voice bars (11 on their own) and ×. No time until 10 s, then the time with 7 bars. |
| Hands-free | Bars, the time, Stop and ×. In the last minute before the length limit, "Stops in 0:42" in amber. |
| Transcribing | The quill writing its line, "Transcribing", and ×. |
| Inserted or pasted | The quill's line completing, "Inserted" or "Pasted", and, unless the confirmation is the brief one, the app's name with its icon. |
| Inserted, with words | A two-line card: the quill and "Inserted into Mail", then the first line of what was inserted, at most 360 pt wide. |
| Copied | The quill, "Copied", the reason ("Password field", "App changed", …) and a ⌘V keycap. |
| A problem | Graphite, a caution or microphone glyph, one line such as "Couldn’t insert. Saved in History.", and one action (Copy, Open Settings). |
| Discarded | Graphite, "Discarded" and Undo while it is available. |

The voice bars are 2.5 pt wide with 2.5 pt gaps, 3 pt at silence and up to 18 pt, so silence reads as a row of dots. Each bar shows the level from a few updates ago, older toward the ends, so the voice ripples out from the centre.

**How long results stay** (`OverlayPresentation.swift`): a brief "Inserted" 0.6 s; inserted with words 2 s; pasted 2 s; copied 4 s; no speech 2 s; a problem 6 s; discarded with Undo 5 s; a busy notice 1.6 s. Moving the pointer over a result holds it, and at least 1 s remains after the pointer leaves. A pointer that was already resting there does not hold it.

**After inserting** (`DictationResultPresentation.swift`). By default a successful insertion is a brief check, with words for the first 20 dictations and always for a paste, because Steno cannot confirm where a paste landed. "Always say where" and "Show nothing" are the other choices; with "Show nothing" VoiceOver still announces the insertion. Copies and problems always show. The island never shows the words of a copy, a problem, a brief confirmation, or text concealed for a password field.

**The insert moment.** Between listening and its result the island never gets narrower than it was while listening, so the result grows out of the listening shape rather than jumping in.

**Motion** (`WaveformOverlayPresenter.swift`)

| Movement | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Capsule appears | At once, full size, no animation |
| Notch emerges | Spring, 0.42 s, bounce 0.2 |
| Notch retracts | 0.28 s, no bounce |
| Width change for a result | Spring, 0.35 s, no bounce |
| Controls that appear | Fade in 0.09 s after the shape starts to move |
| Forest to graphite | Fill cross-fades over 0.15 s |
| Disappear (capsule) | Cross-fade 0.15 s |
| Reduce Motion | Each shape change becomes a 0.14 s cross-fade; bars keep their shape and show level by opacity, at most ten times a second |

Every shape change is one path animation run by the render server; the app does no work per frame.

### Main window

A unified toolbar holds a segmented control for Today, History and Insights (⌘1 to ⌘3), the status pill and a Settings button (`MainWindow.swift`, `ContentView.swift`). The pages share one column, at most 960 pt wide, so their edges line up in any window. The window's minimum is 960 × 640 pt and it opens at 1,120 × 760 (`MainWindow.swift`); the minimum is held with layout constraints on the content view, because the window's own minimum size was being reset by SwiftUI (`WindowConfigurator.swift`).

The status pill reports one state: Listening, Transcribing, Microphone off, No speech model, Copy only, a shortcut key off, Downloading, or Ready (`WindowStatus.swift`). On Today it hides for Ready, Listening, Transcribing and Microphone off, which the page already says. Clicking the pill when it shows a problem opens the Settings page that fixes it. A problem that affects every page appears as a strip under the toolbar with one named button.

### Today

Today answers two questions: can I dictate now, and what did I last say.

**The Dictate stage**. At rest the top of the page is one forest glass button, 20 pt corner radius: the quill on a cream disc, "Dictate", a line that names the working shortcuts as keycaps ("Click, or hold ⌥ Option in any app and speak"), and "Ready · " with the microphone's name beside a mint dot. Clicking it dictates into the window and copies the words when you stop. It starts with Space only while it has the keyboard focus, and its line then begins "Press Space, or", so a stray Space elsewhere never opens the microphone. With microphone access off it becomes "Microphone access is off" with Open System Settings.

**The live stage**. The same glass becomes the live stage while a dictation runs: the quill listening, writing, then written; "Listening 0:14 · into Mail", or "· copied when you stop" for a dictation started in the window; the words at 22 pt, settled words in cream and words that may still change in the softer subtext color, at most four lines. Discard answers Esc and Stop answers Space from anywhere in the window. While transcribing it says "The microphone is off." and offers Cancel.

**Folding**. Starting a dictation and the stage's own changes are instant. When the result is in, the words settle to cream, hold for 1.1 s, and the stage folds into the latest dictation's card with a 0.35 s spring and no bounce; under Reduce Motion it is a 150 ms cross-fade. A discard or a recording with no speech turns the stage into a note with one action, which leaves after 6 s. A failed dictation is shown once, in the window's strip, not again on Today.

**The day**. Below the stage, one panel holds the date, the day's dictation and word counts, and Show in History. The latest dictation comes first: where it went and when, its words as plain text on the panel, then Copy, Fix a Word and How It Was Written. A dictation over 12 lines shows Show All. "Earlier today" lists up to 12 more, two lines each, followed by "N more in History" when there are more. Today is a glance; History holds the rest. With nothing dictated yet, the page shows a small quiet quill and one line saying each dictation is saved in History on this Mac.

### History

History is a list and a reader side by side.

- **The list** is a 320 pt panel. Its search field sits at the top of the list rather than in the toolbar; the magnifier opens the filter (All, Inserted, Copied, Not Inserted) and Delete All History…, and an active filter is named at the field's edge so the list is never filtered silently (`HistoryRules.swift`). Dictations are grouped under day titles. The list is one Tab stop and the arrow keys move the selection.
- **The reader** shows where the dictation went and when, its words as plain text, a plain sentence when it was not inserted or was cleaned up again, and Copy, Fix a Word, Clean Up Again and Delete as text buttons that wrap in a narrow reader.
- **How it was written** says in one sentence how long the text took after you stopped, "all on this Mac", then each timed step (heard, transcribed, cleaned up, inserted) as a small column with its time and detail. There is no bar: the numbers are the information.
- **Around then** lists up to three dictations from the half hour before the open one, on the same day. **Before cleanup** shows the words as heard.
- Leaving History and coming back returns to the same dictation and filter (`ContentView.swift`). History keeps the latest 1,000 dictations, and its empty state says so (`HistoryStore.swift`).

### Fix a Word

Fix a Word corrects a misheard word in a saved dictation (`FixWord.swift`, `FixableTranscriptEntry.swift`).

- Start it from the Fix a Word button, ⌘E, or the context menu of a row in History. Then click the word in the text, or choose "Choose from a List…", which lists every word of the dictation in order, a repeated word marked by which time it appears. The list exists because VoiceOver and the keyboard alone cannot click a word.
- The form is anchored to the word: Correct spelling, then "Apply to" with **Just this dictation** (the default) or **This and future dictations**, which also adds the pair to Word Corrections and says so. Return fixes, Esc cancels, and Fix is disabled until the spelling changes.
- After a fix, a note under the dictation offers Copy Corrected Text and Undo, and lists show a "Fixed" chip.
- Each word in the form's note is isolated for text direction, so a right-to-left word and its correction keep their order around the arrow.
- Steno's popovers open, resize and close without animation: on macOS 26 an animated popover resize inside layout crashed the app (`FixWord.swift`).

### Insights

Insights reads only the usage ledger, which holds daily totals and never dictation text.

- It opens with one 22 pt sentence on the month: words so far and the typing time saved, counted against typing at 40 words a minute less the time spent speaking. A line under it gives pace and streak where the ledger supports them. When nothing was dictated this month, the sentence counts all time.
- **The last six months** is a calendar of every day in the window (`UsageAnalytics.swift`). Cells are 8 to 28 pt with 4 pt gaps and grow to fill the panel. Days are filled from a solid green ramp in four steps by a quarter of the busiest day each: light `#EEF5F0` `#C4E6CF` `#8FD0A8` `#4FA374` `#2A6B49`, dark `#232826` `#1F4A33` `#2E6E4A` `#4C9E6F` `#7FD3A0`. The ramp is solid because the forest at partial opacity turned sage on white and read as grey. Today is outlined in ink. Arrow keys move a day (up and down) or a week (left and right). The busiest day of the window is named beside the calendar, or under it when the panel is narrow.
- **Where your words went · all time** ranks apps by words with their share at the right. There are no bars, because a bar under every name read as a scroll bar.
- There are no usual hours, because the ledger keeps daily totals only.

### Menu bar

**The icon** (`MenuBarIcon.swift`) is the quill's small cut as an 18 pt template image. A badge sits under the quill's tip when something needs attention. While recording it becomes a filled tile with the quill cut out and its barbs set by the level in four frames, changing at most ten times a second and never under Reduce Motion. While transcribing it is an outlined tile with the nib's line part written.

**The panel** (`MenuBarMenuModel.swift`). Left and right click both open a 290 pt forest panel, 14 pt corner radius, the same in light and dark, with cream text and no rules; groups are separated by a 6 pt gap. The header says what Steno is doing (Ready, Listening, Transcribing, Not ready) and how to start with the shortcuts that are on. Its indicator is a mint dot at rest, an amber dot when Steno cannot dictate, bars that follow the voice while listening and three dots while transcribing. Then Dictate, which starts hands-free, or Stop while recording; a "Microphone access is off" strip with Turn On… when needed; the latest dictation on a card with a copy glyph; Discard while recording, or Undo Discard after a long recording is discarded; Paste Last Dictation, with Earlier and Microphone opening in place rather than as nested menus; then Open Steno, Settings… and Quit Steno. The highlighted row is white at 15%, 20% with an edge under Increase Contrast.

The panel does not take the keyboard until the keyboard is used, so opening it never takes focus from the app being dictated into. It closes on an outside click, Esc, another app coming forward, a Space or screen change, or a command running. Under Reduce Transparency the blur under the forest is removed.

### Settings and the setup guide

Settings is its own window with a 210 pt sidebar on the well fill: a search field, then the groups Dictation, Text and This Mac, with the quill and the version at the foot (`SettingsView.swift`). Rows sit on the plain ground with no boxes or separators; group labels are 11 pt semibold and rows 28 pt tall; the selected row is a neutral fill with no accent or weight change (`SettingsStyle.swift`). Changes are drafts until Save Changes, in a bar that counts the unsaved changes and names them when there are few. Appearance offers System, Light and Dark only; the design has one accent and no line numbers, so neither is offered (`AppearanceSettingsSection.swift`).

The setup guide has three steps: Welcome, with the app icon drawn from the mark; three permissions, each asked for in turn, plus a speech model row only when the model is missing; and a first dictation into a practice box, which ends with the quill written and a line such as "Heard and written in 1.1 seconds, on this Mac.", with the time that dictation took (`OnboardingView.swift`).

## Wording

- **"Saved in History."** A dictation that was not inserted is described with one phrase everywhere: the island ("Not pasted. Saved in History.", "Couldn’t insert. Saved in History."), VoiceOver, Today's note and the list chip ("Not inserted · Saved in History") (`OverlayPresentation.swift`). Empty states say each dictation is saved in History on this Mac.
- **"How It Was Written"** is the one name for the timings section and the action that opens it, in the menu bar, on Today's card, in context menus and in VoiceOver (`DictationCommands.swift`).
- **"Transcribing"** is the word for that step on the island, the stage, the menu bar and the setup guide.
- Buttons and menu items use Mac title case ("Show All 95 Words", "How It Was Written", "Not Inserted"); sentences use sentence case.

## Accessibility commitments

- **Keyboard focus.** The 1.1 controls draw a 2 pt ring when the keyboard focus reaches one, from Tab with Full Keyboard Access or from VoiceOver; a click draws none. On forest glass the ring is mint, because the accent green on glass is 1.4:1 and mint is 5.8:1 or better. The ring is drawn on macOS 14 and later, where the system effect can be switched off; on macOS 13 the system's own ring shows.
- **Menu commands.** Search History (⌘F) is in the Edit menu; Fix a Word… (⌘E) and How It Was Written (⌘I) are in a Dictation menu, enabled for the dictation the page shows; Today, History and Insights (⌘1 to ⌘3) are in the View menu. The page answers the same keys, and the hidden key buttons are not Tab stops (`DictationCommands.swift`, `MainWindow.swift`).
- **Text size.** The windows follow the system text size up to 1.35 times; the island follows it without that cap (see [Type](#type)).
- **Reduce Motion.** Covered per surface above: the island cross-fades, the quill and bars show level by opacity, the stage cross-fades into its card, and the menu bar icon stops changing.
- **Reduce Transparency.** Forest glass in the window becomes solid forest; the island drops its shadow; the menu panel drops its blur.
- **Increase Contrast.** Inks, caution and rings darken or lighten; a panel's edge, its only boundary on a ground of the same color, reaches 3:1; the glass rim rises to 50%; the island's hairline becomes one flat cream line at 60%.
- **VoiceOver.** Each island state carries one announcement, and a silent success is still announced (`OverlayPresentation.swift`, `DictationResultPresentation.swift`). A list row is one element that reads its preview, app, time and any exception. Each calendar day is a button that names the date and what was dictated in words rather than symbols. Decorative quills are hidden.
- **Status is always a word.** Color never carries a state alone: the island, the pill, the chips and the menu header all name it.

## Performance commitments

These rules are in the code. Where a rule names a reason, the slower version was found by measuring.

- The input level reaches the quill and the bars through their layers, never through view state, so a level update at the meter's rate redraws no view (`BrandMarkView.swift`).
- The island's shape changes run on the render server with no per-frame app work.
- Today lists at most 12 earlier dictations, and its lists are recomputed only when the dictations, the hold or the day change. History builds only the rows on screen, from one flat lazy list, and its filtered list and day groups are recomputed only when the dictations, search, filter or day change. A row redraws only when a field it shows changes.
- The Insights calendar is painted in one view, with VoiceOver's day buttons built only when asked for. It replaced about 180 separate buttons that were most of the time Insights took to open.
- The toolbar is rebuilt only when the section or the status inputs change, and the status pill stays in the toolbar on every section, clear where it is not shown, because adding and removing it made the toolbar lay out again on every switch (`ContentView.swift`).
- The menu bar icon changes at most ten times a second (`MenuBarIcon.swift`).

Measured results recorded with the changes, each in a Debug build with 1,008 sample dictations, as instructions retired on the main thread, median of three runs:

| Change | Step measured | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The app's scenes stop observing the controller | Ten app-state changes that show nothing new: Today, History, Insights | 2.05, 1.96, 0.62 billion | 1.59, 1.25, 0.38 billion |
| History's list and Around then skip unchanged redraws | Ten app-state changes on History | 1.64 billion | 1.30 billion |

The first recording after launch used to wait for the system's audio component registrar, measured at 1.6 s, and blocked the main thread for 1.8 s before any audio was captured. Steno now loads the component list at launch, off the main thread, without opening any audio device (`AudioComponentWarmup.swift`).

## Compatibility

- The 1.1 design is the only one. Stored values from development builds for a design choice, the mark, a blue identity, or the island and stage styles are never read, so the app shows green and glass whatever they hold.
- The app does not read or rewrite the saved accent or line-number preferences (`AppearanceSettingsSection.swift`).
- macOS 13 stays supported. The custom focus ring is drawn only where the system allows it, from macOS 14.

## Known limits and open items

- **No dark or tinted app icon.** `Design/Steno.icon/icon.json` has one light gradient fill and no appearance variants, and `AppIcon.appiconset` lists no appearances.
- **The live stage's words are a fixed 22 pt.** They do not follow the system text size.
- **Window text stops growing at 1.35 times.** Larger system text sizes do not enlarge the windows further.
- **Archivo and Azeret Mono still ship but are not drawn.** The app bundles and registers both faces at launch, but no window or island state in the 1.1 design draws them; only the overlay's earlier look, which the app never selects, asks for them. Removing them moved some window text by 1 to 2 pixels at larger text sizes in the render tests, so they stay until that is checked (`Steno/Resources/Fonts`, `DesignSystem.swift`, `WaveformOverlayPresenter.swift`).
- **VoiceOver says copies in a different form from the island.** The island shows "Copied · Password field"; VoiceOver says "Password field. Copied. Press Command V." The facts match; the wording does not (`OverlayPresentation.swift`).
