### Recognition status

The unwanted prompt-derived repetition bug is closed after maintainer testing of the retained-helper correction. Preserve deliberately spoken words. Broader native acceptance remains separate.

Two recognition limitations remain in 1.0: quiet speech after a long silent lead-in can lose words during final VAD preparation, and `small.en` can make word substitutions. The experimental VAD correction is not included in 1.0. The CLI fallback does not include the retained helper's prompt-verification correction.
